# Read the manual

Read the manual without leaving the application, so that you keep your place while you learn how a screen works.

## Steps

1. Click "Manual" in the menu. The Manual page opens on the home page of the manual, which lists the areas of the application as links.
2. Click an area. Its page lists the pages of that area as links.
3. Click a page. It opens with its title, an introduction and sections such as **Steps** and **What you will see**.
4. Click a link to a section to open the page already scrolled to that section.

## What you will see

- The manual is shown in the language of the application. If you change the language, the manual changes with it.
- The pages of an area are listed in the order of the features they describe.

## Good to know

- The manual is read-only: nobody edits it from the application.
- You cannot print it, save it as a file or choose a language different from the one of the application, and it does not link to other websites.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "The manual could not be loaded." | Reload the page later. |
