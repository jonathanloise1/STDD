# Ask a question about the application

Type a question in your own words in the help panel and see the parts of the manual that answer it.

## Steps

1. Click the "Help" button in the page header.
2. Click the "Ask" tab.
3. Type your question in the "Your question" field, for example How do I create a task. It accepts at most 300 characters.
4. Click "Ask". Up to three cards appear at once.
5. Click "Open in the manual" on a card to go to that section.

## What you will see

- Each card shows the page title, the section heading, the first 160 characters of the section and an "Open in the manual" link.
- When no short written answer is given, the cards are headed "Best matches in the manual".
- If the question is about something the application does not do, the panel shows "The manual has nothing on this yet." with the links "Go to the manual home" and "Search the manual for this". The second link opens the search results for the words of your question.

## Good to know

- The cards come from the whole manual, not only from the pages of the current screen, because the answer often lives on another screen.
- The panel shows at most three cards for a question, and a question outside the scope of the application always gets the same fixed reply.
- The question is looked up in the manual of the language of the application. The panel needs no download on your device for this.
- Each question is answered on its own, without memory of the previous ones. The panel does not answer about your own data, such as the tasks you created, and it does not send your question to a person.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Help is unavailable." | The help could not be loaded when you asked. Open the manual later. |
