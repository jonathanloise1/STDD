# Search the manual

Search the manual for a few words to reach the right section without knowing where it is.

## Steps

1. Click "Manual" in the menu.
2. Type a few words in the "Search the manual" field, for example create task.
3. Click "Search". The search results open.
4. Click a result to open that page in the manual.

## What you will see

- The results show at most 10 sections, grouped by page, with the page that matches best first.
- Each result shows the page title, the section heading and the first 160 characters of the section.
- The words that match your search are in bold, including other forms of the same word.

## Good to know

- A search for "tasks" also finds sections that say "task". Words that carry no meaning, such as "the" or "a", are ignored.
- The search looks in the manual of the language of the application.
- It searches only the manual, not your data such as task titles. You cannot filter results by area, or save or share a search.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Nothing found in the manual." | Try other words, or click "Go to the manual home" and browse the areas. |
| "Help is unavailable." | The manual could not be loaded for the search. Open the manual later. |
