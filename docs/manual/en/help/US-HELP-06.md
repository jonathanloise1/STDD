# Say whether the answer helped

Tell the help whether it solved your problem, so that the manual improves where it falls short.

## Steps

1. Ask a question in the "Ask" tab of the help panel.
2. Under the results, find the question "Did this help?". It is also shown under the fixed reply "The manual has nothing on this yet."
3. Click "Yes" or "No".

## What you will see

- The message "Thanks for your feedback." appears.
- The cards stay as they were: clicking "No" does not change what the panel shows.

## Good to know

- A "No" makes your question available to the administrators of the organization, in the list of unanswered questions, without your name. A "Yes" is not passed on to anyone.
- You cannot add a comment, change your vote after you gave it, or get a reply from the administrators.
