# Upload the organization logo

Add a logo to your organization, so it is easy to recognise.

## Before you start

- Only Admins can do this.

## Steps

1. In the menu, click "Settings".
2. Click "Edit".
3. Click the area "Click to upload logo" and choose an image file. It can be JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB.
4. Click "Save Changes".

## What you will see

- The message "Logo uploaded successfully", and the logo replaces the placeholder.
- After saving, the Organization page shows the logo instead of the first two letters of the name.

## Good to know

- You cannot remove a logo once it is set, and you cannot crop or resize the image.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Only image files are allowed" | Choose a JPG or PNG image. |
| "Maximum file size is 5MB" | Choose a smaller image. |
| "The service did not answer. Try again in a few minutes." | Choose the image again later. |
