# Open the task list from the dashboard

Go from the dashboard straight to the task list, without searching the menu.

## Steps

1. In the menu, click "Dashboard".
2. In the "Recent Tasks" section, click "View All". You can also click any row of the table.

## What you will see

- The Tasks page opens with the full list of tasks.

## Good to know

- "View All" is there whether or not the organization has tasks.
- Clicking a row opens the list, not the task itself, and you cannot create a task from the dashboard.
