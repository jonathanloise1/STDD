## Models

No model is part of the repository and none has been downloaded. The candidate models, their licences and the
checks before use are recorded in `docs/guidelines/models.md` (sections 6 and 7), `training/assistant/config/candidates.json`,
`training/investigator/config/candidates.json` and, once a model is recorded, in its model card under `models/`.
The embedding model's card wording must be checked by whoever uses it: the card of the model chosen in models.md
describes itself in terms that do not match the Apache-2.0 metadata, and the owner reviews the licence before the first
build.
